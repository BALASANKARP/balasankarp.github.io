Gas meter corrosion inspection

Client project for a US utility, 2025 to now

Gemini 2.5 Flash, Vertex AI, Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, BigQuery, Cloud Storage, ArcGIS, Python

Problem
Crews photograph each gas meter (one to seven photos) and supervisors reviewed every photo by hand for corrosion, which was slow and varied between reviewers.
Approach
An hourly Google Cloud pipeline. Cloud Scheduler starts a Cloud Run job that loads ArcGIS survey records into BigQuery and photos into Cloud Storage, then Pub/Sub triggers an inference service that sends each photo to Gemini 2.5 Flash with few-shot examples. It classifies paint loss and pitted pipe and writes the result back to ArcGIS.
Key decisions
Chose few-shot Gemini over training a YOLO detector, because reliable labeled data didn’t exist and the existing field flags disagreed with each other. A review app shows supervisors only the photos where the model and the field flag disagree. Their decisions refine the prompts and build a verified dataset for future detector training.
Outcome
Runs over 900K+ survey records in 7 regions, replacing a manual pass over every photo with review of the disagreements only.

GST Copilot

A RAG assistant for Indian GST law, 2025

Python, Gemini, text-embedding-004, Qdrant, Streamlit, Docker, GCP

Problem
GST law is spread across Acts, CBIC circulars, orders and case laws, and a correct answer depends on which source outranks another.
Approach
A retrieval-augmented assistant over about 9,000 documents, stored as 170K+ text chunks in Qdrant with text-embedding-004 and answered by Gemini. Each document type is split differently (Acts by section, circulars with overlapping windows, judgments as a summary plus detail), and every chunk carries its source label.
Key decisions
Retrieved passages are re-weighted by legal authority, so an Act outranks a circular, and every answer cites its sources. Requirements and test questions came from a team of chartered accountants.
Status
Working prototype. Next: a CA-reviewed golden question set, RAGAS evaluation, and hybrid keyword plus vector search.

Field operations forecasting and risk scoring

Client project for a US utility, 2025 to now

Prophet, SARIMA, LightGBM, Monte Carlo, Streamlit, Gemini, BigQuery

Problem
Planning teams needed to know how many tickets were coming and which excavation requests put gas lines at risk.
Approach
Weekly ticket forecasts four weeks ahead for each county and work type, choosing between Prophet, SARIMA and LightGBM by backtested error. A staffing planner using Prophet and Monte Carlo simulation gives technician needs for 11 service areas as P50, P80 and P95 scenarios, with Gemini-written explanations of the gaps. A risk score for 811 dig tickets across 10 Texas counties combines census density, GIS pipeline attributes and proximity analysis.
Outcome
Operations gets a four-week demand view and automatic email alerts for emergency tickets.
